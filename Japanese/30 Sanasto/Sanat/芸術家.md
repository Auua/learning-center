## Esimerkit
- この芸術家の作品には、苦笑いがかくせません。 — I can't hide my wry smile at this artist's works.
- その芸術家は、弟子に留守番をたのんで、ヨーロッパに留学しました。 — The artist asked his disciple to look after his house while he was away and left for Europe to study.
- 再来月のマラソン大会、芸術家達と一緒に参加するってのはどう？ — How about running a marathon with a bunch of artists the month after next?
