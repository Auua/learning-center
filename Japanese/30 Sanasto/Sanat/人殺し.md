## Esimerkit
- あいつ、人殺しのくせに、仏教となんだぜ。 — Even though he is a murderer, he is also a Buddhist.
- 計算ずくの人生なんてつまらないと思って好き勝手していたら、いつの間にか人殺しになってたんだ。 — I thought it wouldn't be fun to lead a calculating life, so I'd just do whatever I please, but I ended up becoming a murderer before I knew it.
- おい、昨日駅でお前が彼女といるのを見かけたぜ。お前の彼女、人殺しとも付き合えないぐらい不細工だな。 — Hey, I saw you with your girlfriend at the station yesterday. She’s so plain, she probably couldn’t even get a date with a murderer.
