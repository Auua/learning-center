## Esimerkit
- 私立の大学は学費が高い。 — Tuition for the private university is expensive.
- 私立のようちえんはクリスマスの日はお休みだよ。 — The private kindergartens are closed on the Christmas Day.
- ここを出て、前の通りを左の方へ三つ目の交さ点まで歩くと、その私立病院に着きますよ。 — Go out of here and walk left along the street until you come to the third intersection and you'll get to the private hospital.
