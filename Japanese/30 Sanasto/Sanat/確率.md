## Esimerkit
- 天気予報は当たる確率が高くなりましたね。 — The chance that the weather forecast is right has increased.
- コウイチがあやまってこの穴に落ちる確率は、20%ぐらいだろうね。 — The probability of Koichi accidentally falling into this pit is at about 20%.
- コウイチが友情に厚い人である確率はどのぐらいだと思いますか？ — What's the likelihood of Koichi being someone who's kind to his friends?
