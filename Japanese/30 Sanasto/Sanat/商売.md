## Esimerkit
- 彼の商売は儲かっているな。 — His business is profitable.
- 一しょに商売して一もうけしようぜ！ — Let's go into business and make a fortune together!
- 最近はあのコンビニのせいで、商売あがったりだね。 — My business has dried up recently because of that convenience store.
