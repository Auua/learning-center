## Esimerkit
- 工事の音がうるさい。 — The construction site is noisy.
- きのうてつ夜で仕事だったんですが、朝から工事の音がうるさくてぜんぜんねむれてないんです。 — I worked all night last night, but I haven’t been able to sleep at all because of the loud construction that started this morning.
- 町中で一日中工事しているので、通行止めだらけでいやになるよ。 — Because there's construction going on all day, all over town, there are roads closed everywhere and I'm sick and tired of it.
