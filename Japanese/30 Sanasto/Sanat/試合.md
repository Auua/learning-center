## Esimerkit
- 試合の結果を早く知りたい。 — I want to know soon the results of the game.
- 良かったらもう一試合しませんか？ — If you don't mind, why don't we play another game together?
- けん道の試合の結果はどうでしたか？ — What was the outcome of the kendo match?
