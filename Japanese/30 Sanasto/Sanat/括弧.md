## Esimerkit
- 括弧の部分は省略できます。 — The part in parentheses can be omitted.
- 左の括弧を読む時は単に「括弧」と言いますが、右の括弧を読む時は「括弧閉じる」と言います。 — When people read the left bracket, they just say "kakko," but when they read the right bracket, they say "kakko-tojiru," which means "close bracket."
