## Esimerkit
- 銀行は3時まで開いています。 — The bank is open until three o'clock.
- 銀行にお金を下ろしに行かないといけないんです。 — I have to go to the bank to withdraw some cash.
- 一円を笑う者は一円に泣くということわざがあるので、銀行に一円をあずけに行くことにしました。 — There is a proverb that says someone who laughs over one yen will eventually cry over one yen, so I decided to go to the bank to put one yen in my account.
