## Esimerkit
- 彼は地理に詳しいの。 — He's knowledgeable in geography.
- 一人で地理のべん強をするのもすきですが、友だちとべん強する方がたのしいです。 — I like studying geography by myself too, but it’s more fun to study with my friend.
- アメリカ人の友人は地理が全くのにが手で、さい近まで日本が中国の首とだとおもっていたらしい。 — My American friend is not good at geography at all, so I heard that she used to think Japan was the capital of China until very recently.
