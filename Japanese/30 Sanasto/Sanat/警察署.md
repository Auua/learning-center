## Esimerkit
- 警察署へ行って、何の得があるの？ — What's the use in going to the police station?
- 財布をひろったので警察署にとどけたら、指もんをとられました。 — I found a wallet and brought it to the police station, then they took my fingerprints.
- 日本の警察署に英語が喋れる人が常駐してたらいいのにな。 — I wish there was always someone who could speak English in police stations in Japan.
