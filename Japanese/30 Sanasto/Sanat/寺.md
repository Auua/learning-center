## Esimerkit
- あそこに古いお寺があります。 — There's an old temple there.
- 昨夜、あのお寺で変死体が発見されたらしいよ。 — Apparently, the body of someone who died accidentally was found at the temple last night.
- 子どものころ、寺のとなりに住んでいたので、仏教の勉強には大変都合が良かったんです。 — I lived next to a temple when I was a child and it was really convenient for studying Buddhism.
