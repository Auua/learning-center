## Esimerkit
- フグは最近ちょっと暴走気味だよね。 — Fugu has been running a little wild recently, hasn’t he?
- 暴走しているコウイチをビエトはやさしくつつみこんだ。 — Viet gently embraced Koichi who had been out of control.
