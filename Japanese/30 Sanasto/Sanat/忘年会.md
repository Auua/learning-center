- **EN:** year-end party; Year End Party, End Of Year Party; year-end (dinner) party

## Esimerkit
- 明日は会社の忘年会があります。 — The company will have a year-end party tomorrow.
- 今日の忘年会であった出来事は、忘れる事にします。 — As for what happened at today's year end party, I'll just let it go.
- 元々忘れっぽいんで、私には「忘年会」は必要ありません。 — I'm already really forgetful, so I don't need a so-called "forget the year party."
