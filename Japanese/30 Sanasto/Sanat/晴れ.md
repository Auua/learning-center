## Esimerkit
- 明日の天気は晴れです。 — Tomorrow's weather will be sunny.
- 天気予ほうでは、明日も明後日も明々後日も晴れでしょうって言ってたよ。 — The weather forecast said that it will be clear tomorrow, a day after tomorrow, and two days from now.
- 予ほうは「晴れ後くもり」だし、かさはいらないんじゃない？ — The forecast says "clear weather, with clouds later on," so I don't think you need an umbrella.
