## Esimerkit
- 大変だったけど、みんなで決めた計画を貫徹することができた。 — It was hard, but we were able to carry through with the plan we had decided on together.
- 十年前の夢を貫徹できなかったことを残念に思います。 — I regret that I couldn't realize the dream that I had ten years ago.
- 初志貫徹の心構えで頑張ります。 — I’ll do my best to prepare to accomplish my original goals.
