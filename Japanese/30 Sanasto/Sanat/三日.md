## Esimerkit
- 手紙が届くのに三日かかりました。 — It took three days for the letter to arrive.
- 二人は、せんせん月の三日にどこであいましたか？ — Where did those two people meet on the third, two months ago?
- 三日も水をのんでいません。 — I haven't had water for three days.
