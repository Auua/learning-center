**Conversational expressions**

| 表現 | English |
|---|---|
| ～っていうのはどうですか。 | How about....<br>*When asked for advice, this expression indicates that you are merely making a suggestion, leaving it up to the person you are advising whether to adopt it or not.* |
| それも{悪|わる}くないですね。 | Yes, that's not bad either. |
| それもそうですね。 | Yes, I suppose that's right. |
| けど、……。 | But.... |
| それも{悪|わる}くないですけど……。 | That's not a bad idea, either, but....<br>*Giving one's own opinion while acknowledging that the other person's opinion also has merit.* |
