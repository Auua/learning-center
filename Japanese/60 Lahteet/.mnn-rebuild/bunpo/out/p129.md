## Key Learning Points

| Lesson | {話|はな}す・{聞|き}く (Speak and Listen) | {読|よ}む・{書|か}く (Read and Write) |
|---|---|---|
| **Lesson 1** | **お{願|ねが}いがあるんですが**<br>(There's a favor I'd like to ask you, if...) | **{畳|たたみ}**<br>(Tatami) |
| Learning goals | Politely make a request one is hesitant about.<br>Express gratitude. | Read a passage while noting where the history and merits of the things it discusses are described. |
| Grammar points | 1．～てもらえませんか・～ていただけませんか・～てもらえないでしょうか・～ていただけないでしょうか | 2．～のようだ・～のような～・～のように…<br>3．～ことは／が／を<br>4．～を～と{言|い}う<br>5．～という～<br>6．いつ／どこ／{何|なに}／だれ／どんなに～ても |

| **Lesson 2** | **{何|なん}のことですか**<br>(What does it mean?) | **{外来語|がいらいご}**<br>(Foreign Loanwords) |

| Learning goals | Explain a situation and apologize politely.<br>Ask politely for something to be changed. | Guess what a text is about by looking at a graph. |

| Supplementary points | ＊～の～<br>＊…ましたら、…・…まして、… | |
| **Lesson 5** | **どう{行|い}ったらいいでしょうか**<br>(How do I get to...?) | **{地図|ちず}**<br>(Maps) |
