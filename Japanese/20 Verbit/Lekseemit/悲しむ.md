## Esimerkit
- 父は友だちの死を悲しんでいます。 — My father is grieving because of his friend's death.
- かの女は失れんしてとても悲しんでいます。 — She got dumped and she's very sad.
- 人間だけではなく、動物も仲間の死を悲しみます。 — Animals, as well as humans, are saddened by the deaths of their friends.
