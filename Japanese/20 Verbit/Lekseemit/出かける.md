## Esimerkit
- 母は、デパートへかいものに出かけました。 — Mom went out shopping at the department store.
- おっとが子どもをつれて出かけたので、今日はドラクエ三まいです。 — Since my husband went out with kids, I can play Dragon Quest as much as I want.
- 日曜日は家族で海に出かけるの。 — I’m going out to the beach with my family on Sunday.
