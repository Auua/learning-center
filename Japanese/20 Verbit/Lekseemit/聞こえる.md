## Esimerkit
- 隣の部屋からテレビの音が聞こえる。 — I can hear the sound of the television from the next room.
- 聞こえたんだったら、ちゃんと返事してよ！ — If you could hear me, you should've said something!
- オナラ、聞こえちゃったよね？ — You heard my fart, didn't you?
