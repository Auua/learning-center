## Esimerkit
- トーフグの社長になって、コウイチを見返してやるぞ！ — I will be the Tofugu president and show Koichi, who looked down on me, what I can do.
- 子猫にイワシをあげたら、「本当にいいの？」って感じの顔でマジマジと見返してきたのがマジかわいかった。 — When I gave the kitten some sardines, she looked back at me with a face that was like, "Is this really for me?!" and it was super cute.
- 答案は提出する前に見返したほうがいい。 — You should look back over your test before you submit it.
