## Esimerkit
- コウイチは仕事中、たまに想像できないような動きをする。 — Every once in a while Koichi will move in an unimaginable way while he's working.
- てらや神社をめぐってごしゅいんちょうにごしゅいんを集める仕事って、想像できる？ — Can you imagine having a job where you visit temples and shrines to collect their red ink stamps in a book?
- もし指を喉に突っ込む以外でゲロを吐く方法を学びたいなら、裸のコウイチを想像するといいよ。 — If you want to learn how to vomit without sticking your finger down your throat, just imagine Koichi naked.
