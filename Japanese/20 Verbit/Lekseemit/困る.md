- **FI:** olla pulassa, joutua vaikeuksiin
- **EN:** be at a loss, in a jam, in trouble; To Be Distressed, To Be Troubled, To Be Perplexed, To Be Annoyed; be in trouble, have a problem; to have difficulty [trouble], to be in [get] into trouble, to be embarrassed

## Esimerkit
- ケータイをなくして困っています。 — I'm in a jam because I lost my cell phone.
- タンがのどに絡んで困っています。 — Phlegm is stuck in my throat and it's annoying me.
- 日本語を勉強していますが、名詞や助詞などの意味が分からなくて困っています。 — I am studying Japanese, but I am in trouble because I don't understand the meanings of nouns, particles, and so on.
