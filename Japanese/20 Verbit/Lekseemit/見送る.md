## Esimerkit
- 彼が外国に行くのを見送りました。 — I saw him off on his trip overseas.
- 空こうまで見送るよ。 — I'll go to the airport to see you guys off.
- 見送る方と見送られる方って、どっちの方がつらいんだろうね。 — I wonder which is harder, being the person who sees someone off or being one who is seen off by someone.
