## Merkitys
- **FI:** sotkea, jättää sikin sokin
- **EN:** scatter, disarrange; To Scatter; to scatter about, to leave messed up, to put in disorder
- **Ääni:** ![[anki_bf32002bfd78949ba7b7327732682b73.mp3]]

## Esimerkit
- 部屋を散らかさないでください。 — Please don't mess up the room.
- 私の部屋には脱ぎっぱなしの服が床に散らかっています。掃除しても、次の日にはまたすぐ散らかしちゃうんだよね。 — There are dirty clothes scattered all over the floor in my room. Even if I cleaned them up, it would just get messy again the next day.
